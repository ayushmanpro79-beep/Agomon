## How crowd prediction works (citable facts — from /about)
- 48 slots × 30 min; weights: 4-7AM Very Low 0.18, 8-11AM Moderate 0.55, 12-4PM High 0.82, 5-8PM Peak 1.0, 9-11PM High 0.92, 12-3AM Low 0.35; 12-2PM lunch dip -28-32%
- Cluster: pandals within 1km (1.0), 0.5-1km (0.6), 1-2km (0.3), capped at 4
- POI: South City/Quest/Acropolis/New Market/Park eateries/Esplanade + nearest metro within 3km (malls 10AM-9PM, eateries till 11PM)
- Area base + streets + rating (3.5→5 adds up to 10 pts)
- Formula: crowd = cluster×0.30 + POI×0.35 + streets×0.15 + time×0.20 → 5-98% + rating jitter; source code in src/lib/crowd.ts
